What the Grades Mean

AA (the goal)

The standard most websites must meet. Normal text needs 4.5 : 1. Big text (about 24px, or 19px bold) needs 3 : 1.

AAA (extra credit)

Even easier to read. Normal text needs 7 : 1. Big text needs 4.5 : 1.

Why it matters

Low vision, old screens, phones outside in the sun. Good contrast helps everyone, not just some people.
