Color Basics

Everything else in Color Lab builds on these ideas. Play with each one before moving on.

1. Screens Mix Light

Every pixel on a screen is three tiny lights: red, green, and blue. Mixing light is different from mixing paint. The more light you add, the brighter it gets.

Red, Green, Blue (RGB)

  • Red + green = yellow
  • Green + blue = cyan
  • Red + blue = magenta
  • All three at full power = white
  • All three turned off = black

That is why every color in CSS is really just three numbers: how much red, how much green, and how much blue.

3. The Artist's Color Wheel

Art class uses a red, yellow, blue wheel with 12 colors. Every color family has a name, and the relationships on this wheel work the same way on a screen.

Click a slice of the wheel to see its name.

4. Warm and Cool

Split the wheel in half. Warm colors feel close, active, and exciting. Cool colors feel far away, calm, and relaxing. Most good palettes use mostly one side, with a little of the other side as an accent.

Warm

Energy, excitement, happiness, hunger, danger

Cool

Calm, trust, nature, cleanliness, sadness

5. What Colors Make People Feel

Colors send a message before anyone reads a word. These meanings are common in the United States, but they change from culture to culture. White is for weddings in some places and for funerals in others.
